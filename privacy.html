Privacy Policy – Apna Calendar

Last updated: November 2026

Apna Calendar ("the app") is a family calendar for holidays and personal reminders.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal data on our servers. Reminders you add (names, dates, optional phone numbers) are saved only on your own device and are never sent to us.

WhatsApp

If you tap the WhatsApp button, the app opens WhatsApp on your phone with a prefilled message. The message and number are handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

The app does not ask for contacts, location, camera or microphone permissions.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if ads are added), we will update this page and the date above.
